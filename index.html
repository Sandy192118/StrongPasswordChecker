💡 Strong Password Tips

Follow these tips to create a stronger password.

🔢

Use at least 8 characters

Longer passwords are generally harder to guess.

🔠

Mix uppercase and lowercase letters

Use both capital and small letters in your password.

🔢

Add numbers

Include numbers that are not easy to predict.

🔣

Use special characters

Add symbols such as @, #, $, %, or !.

🚫

Avoid common passwords

Avoid passwords such as password123, 12345678, or your name.

🔄

Don't reuse passwords

Use a different password for each important account.
